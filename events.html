Flagship · 28–31 August 2026

Hoop It Up

Invitational State-Level Basketball Tournament

Hoop It Up began as a tribute to National Sports Day, celebrated on the birth anniversary of the legendary Indian hockey icon Major Dhyan Chand: a day to honour sportsmanship, dedication and athletic excellence.

It has grown into a grand affair. It is now Sports Sphere's premier intercollegiate basketball tournament, with high-energy competition and a spectator experience that celebrates basketball culture on campus.

Flagship · 27–31 October 2026

Sports Summit

MIT-WPU Sports Summit is our flagship national-level sporting event, bringing together athletes, universities and colleges from across India. Built on the philosophy of "Sports for Peace", it celebrates competition, teamwork, leadership and excellence.

How to register

Three steps
to game day

When registration opens for an event, a Register button appears next to it in the calendar above. Registration details for each event are also shared on @sportssphere__mitwpu.

See open events

  • 1. Find your event

    Pick an event in the calendar. If registration is open, you'll see a Register button.

  • 2. Register and get verified

    The button opens the registration form for that event. Fill it in before registration closes.

  • 3. Get ready

    Watch for your confirmation, then follow @sportssphere__mitwpu for fixtures, results and live updates.
