Get in the game

How to make
the team

Selection trials for the university teams run at the start of every season (usually in August). Check the calendar for the dates and the registration form.

See trial dates
Who can try out?

Every enrolled MIT-WPU student, from any faculty, programme or year.

Can other colleges take part?

Yes. Hoop It Up and the Sports Summit are intercollegiate events that bring in teams from across the state and the country.

What happens after selection?

Selected athletes join the Athlete Induction Programme, then train and compete for MIT-WPU through the season.

Are there women's events?

Yes. Football, basketball, volleyball, badminton and table tennis all run men's and women's competition, and there's a dedicated women's wrestling championship.
