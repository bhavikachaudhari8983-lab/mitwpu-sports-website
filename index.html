Who we are

More than
a game.

Sports Sphere is MIT-WPU's official sports management platform, delivering professionally managed events that promote excellence, engagement and the spirit of sport.

From selection trials to the national-level Sports Summit, we run a year-round calendar backed by state-of-the-art infrastructure and professional coaching, nurturing athletes through training and competition.
