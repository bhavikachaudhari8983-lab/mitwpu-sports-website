About us

We are
Sports Sphere.

MIT-WPU's official sports management and event platform, uniting athletes, institutions and brands through the culture of collegiate sport.

Our story

Sport is in
our DNA

MIT World Peace University is a leading multidisciplinary university committed to academic excellence and holistic student development.

Sports Sphere is its official sports management platform. We deliver professionally managed events that promote excellence, engagement and the spirit of sport, from university selection trials to the national-level Sports Summit.

Our athletes train year-round with state-of-the-art infrastructure and professional coaching. Today 12 international players represent India across multiple sports, with 26 international medals between them.

12International players
What we do

The department's
responsibilities

Sports Sphere runs sport at MIT-WPU all year: for the university's athletes, its students and staff, and the colleges that come to compete.

  • Run the season's events

    Hoop It Up, the national-level Sports Summit, interdepartmental sports for students and staff, the marathon, World Yoga Day and more.

  • Pick and prepare university teams

    Selection trials at the start of each season and the Athlete Induction Programme for selected athletes.

  • Train athletes year-round

    Infrastructure and professional coaching that support athletes through training and competition.

  • Celebrate achievement

    The National Level Sports Award Ceremony and recognition of our athletes' results.

  • Build partnerships

    Working with institutions, sponsors and industry to grow university sport.

Our vision

To become India's leading university sports platform, fostering competitive excellence, encouraging holistic development, and building long-term partnerships with institutions and industry leaders.

Our mission

To deliver professionally managed sporting events that celebrate talent, promote inclusivity, and strengthen the culture of sport in higher education, through innovation, collaboration and excellence.

Our objectives
  1. Foster competitive excellence among MIT-WPU's athletes.
  2. Encourage holistic development through sport for every student.
  3. Deliver professionally managed, inclusive sporting events.
  4. Strengthen the culture of sport in higher education.
  5. Build long-term partnerships with institutions and industry.

Our journey

History &
milestones

  1. 1983

    A sporting legacy begins

    Sport has been part of campus life since MIT-Pune was founded.

  2. 2004

    SUMMIT is born

    The first SUMMIT national-level inter-engineering sports meet is held.
